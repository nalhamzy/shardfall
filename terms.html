SHARDFALL: Slice of Joy is provided by Ideal Intelligence for personal entertainment. The free game includes 36 Adventure levels, Daily and Zen.

Deluxe

The optional Deluxe purchase adds six cosmetic blade palettes and six Mastery boss remixes. It is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal. The store displays the current localized price before you confirm payment. Cosmetics do not improve collision rules or scoring power. All purchased content is included in the app; no future content is promised.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Use Restore purchases with the same store account. An Apple purchase does not transfer to Google Play, or vice versa. Refunds are managed by the applicable store; refunded access may be removed.

An internet connection is needed to buy, restore or verify a new purchase. Previously verified access may be available offline through the purchase service's cache. A cancelled, pending or unverified payment does not unlock Deluxe until purchase access is verified.

Local progress

Game progress is stored on this device and is separate from purchase ownership. Uninstalling or clearing storage may erase progress. Restore purchases restores Deluxe access, not your level progress. No cloud save or cross-platform progress transfer is offered.
