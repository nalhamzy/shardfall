Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

SHARDFALL: Slice of Joy is made by Ideal Intelligence. You can play the free Adventure, Daily and Zen modes without creating an account.

On your device

Progress, scores, fruit, cosmetic choices and sound/accessibility settings are stored locally. They are not uploaded to a leaderboard. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may erase this progress. Purchase restoration does not restore your local game progress.

Purchase service and optional Deluxe

Deluxe is a one-time purchase through Apple or Google. When the mobile app's purchase service is enabled, RevenueCat creates an anonymous app-user identifier and checks purchase access, including at startup or when you return to the app. These checks can occur even if you have not bought Deluxe.

RevenueCat processes purchase records, product and transaction identifiers, that anonymous app-user identifier, and technical information needed to process purchase requests. We use this information to validate access, support restoration, prevent fraud and understand purchase performance. We do not send your gameplay history or scores to RevenueCat, and the app has no separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service.

Apple or Google handles your payment details. We do not receive your card number. The game does not request advertising identifiers, use advertising integrations or track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

Restoration, refunds and retention

Restore purchases uses your current store account. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google. Refund decisions follow your store's rules; a refunded purchase may remove Deluxe access.

The store and RevenueCat may retain purchase records to provide restoration, meet legal obligations and prevent fraud. The game does not impose a separate fixed retention period on those providers. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information we can locate, subject to records that must be retained.

Your choices

There are no ads, subscription renewals, public profiles or social sharing in the game. Music, effects and haptics can be disabled in Settings. To remove local game data, use your device's app-storage controls or remove the app; this may permanently erase progress.

For purchase-data or privacy requests, email nalhamzy@gmail.com. Because purchase access uses an anonymous identifier, we may need a transaction reference to locate your purchase. Never send payment-card details or store passwords. Contacting us also gives us the email address and information you choose to include, which we use to respond to your request.

This website

This static website does not run advertising or analytics scripts and does not set application cookies. Its hosting provider may process normal request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub privacy statement for hosting-related processing.
